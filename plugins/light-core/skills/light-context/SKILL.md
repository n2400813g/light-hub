# Light context

Public facts only. Lines marked CONFIRM must be checked by the owner before this skill goes company-wide.

## The product

- Light is an agentic ERP for multinational businesses: multi-currency bookkeeping, real-time reporting and AI-powered pre-accounting on one integrated ledger.
- Agents handle the repetitive work of accounts payable, accounts receivable and reconciliation.
- Light does the sale, the implementation and the support itself.
- Publicly named customers: Lovable, Legora, Fuse Energy. CONFIRM before naming any other.

## The company

- Offices in London, Copenhagen and Stockholm; remote colleagues elsewhere.
- Values: Simple, Fast, Clear.

## How Light writes

- Simple: short sentences, plain words, no jargon the reader did not use first.
- Clear: say what happens next and who does it.

## Never

- Never quote a price or a discount. Route pricing to Finance (the pricing approval memo workflow, once Finance releases it).
- Never promise an integration, certification or feature that is not on Light's public site. CONFIRM the source of truth for integrations.
- Never name a customer that is not in the list above.
