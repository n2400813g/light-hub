# Light context

Public facts only. Lines marked CONFIRM must be checked by the owner before this skill goes company-wide.

## The product

- Light is an agentic ERP for multinational businesses: multi-currency bookkeeping, real-time reporting and AI-powered pre-accounting on one integrated ledger.
- Agents handle the repetitive work of accounts payable, accounts receivable and reconciliation.
- Light sells, implements and supports its product itself; some implementations are led by Light's implementation partners.
- Publicly named customers: Lovable, Legora, Fuse Energy, and the case studies on [light.inc/customers](https://light.inc/customers) (Tillo, Famly, All Gravy, Dreamdata, Ocean.io, Oper, deepset, causaLens, Officeguru, Paebbl, KeyShot). Use a customer's figures only as that page states them, with the link. CONFIRM before naming any other.

## The company

- Offices in London, Copenhagen and Stockholm; remote colleagues elsewhere.
- Values: Simple, Fast, Clear.

## How Light writes

- Simple: short sentences, plain words, no jargon the reader did not use first.
- Clear: say what happens next and who does it.

## Never

- Never quote a price or a discount. Route pricing to Finance (the pricing approval memo workflow, once Finance releases it).
- Never promise an integration, certification or feature that is not on Light's public site. The integrations list is in the [Help Center](https://light.inc/help/integrations); certifications are on the [Trust Center](https://light.inc/trust-center).
- Never name a customer that is not in the list above. Write "[customer, pending approval]" in its place, even in a draft.
