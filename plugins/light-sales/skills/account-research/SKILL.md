# Account research

1. From HubSpot: the company record, contacts, past meetings, open or lost deals, and any partner who referred them.
2. From public sources: what they sell, countries and entities, recent funding, size of the finance team, current finance stack if stated.
3. Fit: say whether they are multi-entity, multi-currency and growing fast, and which of those you could not confirm.
4. Write three questions for the call that test the biggest unknown.
5. List every source with a link.

If HubSpot isn't available to you, ask for the notes or record to be pasted in. Never fill gaps from memory.

## Never

- Never estimate revenue or headcount without a source; write "unknown".
- Never contact the prospect.

## Examples

1. A company with a HubSpot lost deal from last year: the brief opens with why it was lost.
2. A company referred by a partner: the brief names the partner and says to keep them informed.
3. No public data on entities: fit says "entities: unknown" and question 1 asks about it.
