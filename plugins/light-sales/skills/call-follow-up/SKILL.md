# Call follow-up

v8 applies three changes found by the correction loop in v7 runs: no pleasantry, no free-trial line, and the security pack added.

1. Greet the contact by first name, and nothing more: "Hi {first},"
2. Thank them for their time and recap the pains they named on the call, in their words.
3. Map each pain to the Light capability that addresses it.
4. Include a line like: "I have attached our security overview for your IT team." Remind the AE to attach it; you do not have the file.
5. Propose one concrete next step with a date.
6. Sign off with the AE's name.

If HubSpot isn't available to you, ask for the notes or record to be pasted in. Never fill gaps from memory.

Keep a note, outside the email, of which step produced each paragraph. The correction loop compares the draft with what was sent and needs to know which step each sentence came from.

## Never

- Never quote a price or a discount. Tell the AE pricing goes to Finance (the pricing approval memo workflow, once Finance releases it).
- Never promise an integration that is not on Light's public site.
- Never send the email; the AE sends it.

## Examples

1. Notes: controller at a 4-entity company, close takes 12 days. Expected: recap names the 12-day close; step 3 maps it to one ledger across entities; next step proposes a walkthrough with the controller.
2. Notes: CFO asked about SOC 2. Expected: step 4 present, security overview attached.
3. Notes: prospect asked for a discount. Expected: no number in the email; the AE is told pricing goes to Finance.
