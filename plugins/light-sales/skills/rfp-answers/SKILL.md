# RFP answers

Light's Solutions Engineers write "RFP answers" and handle "objections on integrations, data migration, security, controls and audit trail". Every answer here comes from a public source and links to it.

1. Read every question. Sort them into: security and compliance, integrations, migration and implementation, product, commercial, and other.
2. Answer from these sources only, linking the page for each answer:
   - security and compliance: the [Trust Center](https://light.inc/trust-center) and the [DPA](https://light.inc/dpa);
   - integrations, setup and product: the [Help Center](https://light.inc/help);
   - migration and implementation: the [migration page](https://light.inc/migration);
   - anything else Light says about itself: `light-context`.
3. Answer with what the source says and no more. If it covers only part of the question, answer that part and mark the rest.
4. Mark every unanswered question "[needs a person]" with who should answer it: the Solutions Engineer for product depth, Legal & Compliance for contract and security terms, Finance for anything commercial.
5. Start the reply with a count: answered with a source, needs a person.
6. If the questionnaire asks for audit reports, say they are shared under NDA through the Trust Center request.

## Never

- Never claim a certification the Trust Center does not list. Light holds SOC 2 Type II and SOC 1 Type II; it does not claim ISO 27001 itself, and it states SOX readiness, not SOX compliance.
- Never answer pricing, discounts or payment terms. Pricing goes to Finance.
- Never promise an integration, a timeline or a feature on the roadmap.
- Never send the answers or upload them to a buyer's portal; the Solutions Engineer does.

## Examples

1. "Are you ISO 27001 certified?" Expected: SOC 2 Type II and SOC 1 Type II stated with the Trust Center link; ISO 27001 not claimed, marked "[needs a person]" for Legal & Compliance.
2. "Which systems can you migrate from?" Expected: answered from the migration page with its link.
3. "What is your price per user?" Expected: "[needs a person]", Finance.
