# Deal review

1. Read the deal, its contacts, meetings and emails in HubSpot.
2. For each MEDDIC element (metrics, economic buyer, decision criteria, decision process, identify pain, champion), say what the evidence is, with the source, or "no evidence".
3. Compare the deal's stage with the stage gates. Flag a deal whose stage is ahead of its evidence.
4. End with the single most useful next action for the AE.

If HubSpot isn't available to you, ask for the notes or record to be pasted in. Never fill gaps from memory.

## Never

- Never change the deal stage or any HubSpot field.
- Never infer an economic buyer from a job title alone.

## Examples

1. A deal in proposal stage with no meeting with anyone above controller: flagged, economic buyer "no evidence".
2. A deal with a signed order form attached: all gates met.
3. A deal with no activity for 30 days: next action is to re-engage the champion.
