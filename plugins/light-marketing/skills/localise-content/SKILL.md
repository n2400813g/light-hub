# Localise content

Light's regional marketing roles own "localising our story, content and campaigns". The facts come from `light-context`; this workflow changes how they are said, never what is claimed.

1. Ask for the source text, the market, the audience (CFO, controller, partner, press) and the channel.
2. Keep every claim from the source and from `light-context`. Add none.
3. Write it as a local marketer would: local terms for finance roles and processes, local date and number formats, the formality the market expects (for example "Sie" for a German CFO unless the source is deliberately informal).
4. Mark every claim that depends on local rules, such as VAT, e-invoicing or local GAAP, with "[check locally]". A Solutions Engineer confirms them.
5. Below the draft, list each change that is more than translation (a dropped idiom, a changed example) and why.
6. The draft goes to a native speaker on the team before it is used.

## Never

- Never translate a customer quote or case-study figure without the original next to it. Quotes stay as the customer approved them.
- Never name a customer that `light-context` does not list.
- Never add a local integration, bank, certification or regulation that is not on Light's public site.
- Never publish or send it.

## Examples

1. An English CFO-dinner invite for Copenhagen. Expected: Danish draft, informal address, date written the Danish way, list of changes.
2. A German one-pager mentioning e-invoicing. Expected: formal German and that sentence marked "[check locally]".
3. A Dutch landing page with a customer quote. Expected: the quote shown with the English original beside it.
