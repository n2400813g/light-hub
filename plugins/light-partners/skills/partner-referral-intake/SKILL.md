# Partner referral intake

From Part 1 of the case. The full design is in the Part 1 doc.

1. Extract from the email: the prospect company, contact, partner, and anything the partner said about the need. Use only words that appear in the email.
2. Check HubSpot for the company, open deals, existing customer status and earlier registrations by other partners.
3. If none of the exceptions below applies, route to the partner's usual AE for that territory, and draft the first reply on the partner's own thread for the AE to send.
4. Every referral has an owner and a next deadline from the moment it is read.

If HubSpot isn't available to you, ask for the notes or record to be pasted in. Never fill gaps from memory.

## Exceptions a person decides

- Another partner or an AE already registered the company (channel conflict).
- The company is already a customer.
- The HubSpot match is uncertain.
- The prospect is outside the ideal customer profile.
- A commission payout above the approval threshold.

## Never

- Never email anyone who is not on the partner's thread without approval.
- Never promise a commission amount. For commission questions, say who decides (the exception owner) and draft a holding reply.
- Never invent a deadline, an AE or a territory. If the rules of engagement are not available, write "to confirm".

## Examples

1. A clean intro for a new company: routed, reply drafted, deadline set.
2. An intro for an existing customer: sent to a person, partner told it is being checked.
3. One email naming two prospects: two referrals.
