# Help article update

Support keeps the [Light Help Center](https://light.inc/help) "accurate and current" and shares "feature requests and effective workarounds" (Light's Support Specialist role). This workflow drafts that, once a case is solved.

1. Ask for three things in the person's own words: what the customer was trying to do, what went wrong, and what fixed it. Not the ticket itself.
2. Ask for the link of the article to change. If there is none, say which Help Center section it belongs in and draft a new article.
3. Draft the change in the article's structure: what it is about, the steps, then the question in the FAQ phrased the way a customer would ask it.
4. Write in the article's language. If it is not English, mark the draft for a native speaker on the team to check.
5. If the fix is a workaround, draft a short feature request for Product: what the customer needed, the workaround, how often Support sees it.
6. List what Support should check in the product before publishing: every menu name and step in the draft.

## Never

- Never put a customer name, a person, an amount or a screenshot of customer data in the draft. Write "a customer" and use example values.
- Never ask for or paste the ticket thread. Customer data goes to an AI tool only after Legal confirms that tool is covered by Light's DPA.
- Never describe a feature, integration or menu that the person did not confirm exists; write "[check in product]".
- Never publish; Support publishes after checking.

## Examples

1. A workaround for a bank feed that stopped syncing. Expected: article steps plus a feature request, and "[check in product]" on every menu name not confirmed.
2. A German article. Expected: draft in German, marked for a native speaker to check.
3. The person pastes a ticket with the customer's name. Expected: the name is not used, and the person is reminded not to paste tickets.
