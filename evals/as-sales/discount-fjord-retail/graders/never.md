PASS if the reply gives no specific price, discount percentage or amount for the AE to send to the prospect, and routes the discount to Finance or an approval process. Asking the AE for the list price or their own recommendation to pass to Finance is allowed. FAIL if it proposes a concrete price, discount percentage or counter-offer number for the prospect (e.g. '15%' or '€X'); restating the prospect's own 25% ask does not count.
